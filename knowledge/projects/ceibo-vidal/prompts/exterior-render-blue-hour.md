**Tipo:** Render exterior, vista de fachada  
**Momento:** Hora azul (crepúsculo, 20-30 min después del atardecer)  
**Uso:** Comunicación aspiracional, Instagram, web

## Prompt base

```
Photorealistic architectural render of "Ceibo Vidal" residential building in Buenos Aires at blue hour (dusk, 20-30 minutes after sunset).

Same building as the main render: ground floor with glazed lobby (interior lights visible and warm), 4 upper floors with balconies showing interior warm lighting from apartments, top floor with private terrace.

Camera: Same street-level angle as main render, slightly off-center, eye level.

Lighting: Blue hour. Deep blue sky with residual warm horizon glow. Building facade lit by: warm interior lights visible through windows and balcony doors, subtle exterior accent lighting on the facade, ground floor lobby glowing warmly. Balance between cool blue sky and warm building interior.

Materials: Same as confirmed in material-palette.md. Materials appear deeper and more textural at this light.

Environment: Same Buenos Aires residential street. Soft street lighting. 1-2 pedestrians. Planters and trees visible in silhouette with ambient light.

Style: Ultra-realistic. Magazine-quality architectural photography feel. The building is the beacon of the image against the blue sky.

Quality: 4K resolution.
```
