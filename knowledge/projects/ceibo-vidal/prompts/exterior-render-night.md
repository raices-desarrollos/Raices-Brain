# Prompt: Render exterior nocturno — Ceibo Vidal

**Tipo:** Render exterior, vista de fachada  
**Momento:** Noche  
**Uso:** Instagram, web, material de impacto

## Prompt base

```
Photorealistic architectural render of "Ceibo Vidal" residential building in Buenos Aires at night.

Same building: ground floor with fully lit glazed lobby creating a warm glow on the sidewalk, 4 upper floors with varied warm apartment lighting visible through windows and balcony doors, top floor terrace with subtle terrace lighting.

Camera: Street-level, slightly off-center angle, same framing as main render.

Lighting: Full night. Dark sky. Building lit entirely by its own light: lobby warm glow spilling onto sidewalk, balcony and window lights at varied intensities (some residents home, some not), subtle facade accent lighting highlighting the building's geometry. Street lamps casting soft pools of light on sidewalk.

Materials: Same as confirmed palette. Darker tones at night, with light reflections on glass and metallic railings.

Environment: Quiet residential street at night. Sidewalk with subtle wet reflections if possible. 1 pedestrian at distance. Trees silhouetted.

Style: Ultra-realistic, not dramatic over-lit. Feels like a real photograph taken at night of a beautiful residential building. Quiet, residential, desirable.

Quality: 4K resolution.
```
